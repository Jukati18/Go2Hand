| **Free listings** | No upfront cost — you only pay a 5% fee when a sale completes |
| **Escrow protection** | Payment is held securely; buyers cannot take the device and then reverse a bank transfer |
| **IMEI verification** | Proves your device is clean, which commands higher prices and faster sales |

## 6. Step 3 — Grade Condition & Upload Photos

This is the most important step for building buyer trust. Accurate grading reduces disputes, earns better reviews, and leads to repeat buyers.

### Condition grades

#### A+ — Like New
The device has never been used or was used only a handful of times. It shows absolutely no cosmetic wear.

**Requirements to list as A+:**
- Zero scratches under any lighting
- Screen is perfect with no marks
- Original accessories ideally included
- Battery health 95% or above

#### A — Excellent
Light, everyday handling marks that are only visible under close inspection or harsh overhead lighting.

**Requirements to list as A:**
- Possible micro-scratches on back (not visible from 30cm away)
- Screen in perfect condition
- All buttons, ports, and cameras working perfectly
- Battery health typically 85–95%

#### B — Good
Normal use marks visible to the naked eye, but nothing that affects the use or screen.

**Requirements to list as B:**
- Light scratches on back, sides, or corners
- Screen crack-free; may have surface wear
- Full functionality
- Battery health typically 75–85%

#### C — Fair
Noticeable cosmetic wear consistent with heavy regular use.

**Requirements to list as C:**
- Visible scratches, scuffs, or possible small dents
- Screen must be crack-free (cracks require explicit disclosure regardless of grade)
- All core functionality intact
- Battery health typically 65–80%

---

> ### ⚠️ Important: Mandatory Disclosures
>
> Regardless of the grade you select, you **must explicitly disclose** in the description:
> - Any screen cracks, even hairline ones
> - Any dents that create a gap in the chassis
> - Any dead pixels or screen burn-in
> - Any non-original parts (replaced screen, third-party battery)
> - Any functional faults, even intermittent ones
>
> Failure to disclose these gives the buyer valid grounds for a dispute and full refund.

---

### Battery health

For smartphones, enter the battery health percentage. To find it:

**iPhone:**  
Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging → Maximum Capacity

**Samsung / Android:**  
Settings → Battery → Battery usage (or use a free app like AccuBattery for an accurate reading)

**MacBook:**  
Apple Menu → System Information → Power → Condition and Full Charge Capacity

### Uploading photos

A minimum of **5 photos** is required. Listings with 8–10 photos sell measurably faster.

**Required shots:**
1. Front of device (screen on, showing wallpaper or home screen)
2. Front of device (screen off, showing any marks or reflections clearly)
3. Back panel
4. Right side
5. Left side (or bottom — show all ports)

**Strongly recommended:**
6. Any scratches or wear areas, photographed in natural light
7. The IMEI/serial number label (back of device or Settings screen)
8. Accessories included (charger, cable, box, earphones)

**Photo quality tips:**
- Use natural daylight, not flash
- Place device on a neutral background (white table, grey cloth)
- Clean the screen and lens before shooting
- Focus on the device, not your background
- Do not use internet stock photos — buyers will notice and lose trust

**To reorder photos:** Drag and drop thumbnails to rearrange. The first photo is your cover image and appears in search results — make it your best shot.

### iCloud and Carrier status

Before listing, ensure:

**iCloud Lock (iPhone):**  
Go to Settings → tap your name → scroll to bottom → Sign Out. Remove your Apple ID completely before shipping (not just before listing).

**Google Account Lock (Android):**  
Settings → Accounts → Remove your Google account. Factory reset the device after removing the account.

**Carrier lock:**  
If your device is network-locked, select **Locked** and name the carrier. Locked devices sell for less but still sell — buyers on that carrier will purchase them.

Go2Hand analyses your device model and condition grade to suggest a market-rate price range:

│  Min $XXX  │ Suggested $XXX│  Max $XXX  │

Enter what the device sold for new. Go2Hand uses this to calculate the discount percentage shown to buyers — a prominent "Save 35%" badge significantly increases click-through rates.

The payout breakdown is shown live as you type your price.

## 8. IMEI & Serial Verification

Every listing on Go2Hand requires an IMEI or serial number check. This protects buyers from unknowingly purchasing stolen devices — and protects you from accusations of selling one.

### Why it matters for sellers

A clean IMEI verification badge on your listing:
- Increases buyer confidence → more purchase conversions
- Allows you to price 5–15% higher than unverified listings
- Protects you if a buyer later claims the device was stolen (you have a timestamped clean check)

### How to find your IMEI or Serial

**iPhone IMEI:**
- Dial `*#06#` on the phone
- Settings → General → About → IMEI
- On the original box (barcode label)
- On the SIM tray (on some models)

**Android IMEI:**
- Dial `*#06#`
- Settings → About Phone → IMEI
- On the box or under the battery cover (older models)

**Laptop / Tablet Serial Number:**
- On a sticker on the bottom of the device
- System Information / About This Mac
- On the original box

### Running the verification check

In Step 3 of your listing, the IMEI / Serial input field appears. Type or paste your number — dashes and spaces are automatically removed. Click **"Check"**.

**Results:**

| Result | Meaning | Can I list? |
|---|---|---|
| ✓ **Clean** | Not reported stolen or blacklisted | ✅ Yes — badge appears on listing |
| ⚠️ **Flagged** | Found in stolen device database | ❌ No — contact Go2Hand support |
| ✗ **Invalid Format** | Number entered incorrectly | Re-enter the correct number |

> **If your device comes back flagged and you believe this is an error:** Contact Go2Hand support at support@go2hand.vn with proof of purchase (receipt, box, carrier documentation). False positives do occur — particularly with devices purchased internationally or from carrier promotions.

## 10. When You Get a Sale

When a buyer completes checkout, you will receive a notification and your order will appear in **Dashboard → Orders**.

### What happens immediately

1. The buyer's payment is held in escrow by Stripe (not yet paid to you)
2. Your listing status changes from **Active** to **Sold**
3. You receive a notification with the buyer's shipping address

### Your obligation as a seller

You must:
- Ship the device within **3 business days** of the order being placed
- Add a valid tracking number in your order dashboard
- Use a reliable courier service (J&T Express, ViettelPost, GHTK, GHN, or Ninja Van)
- Pack the device safely (see [Section 11](#11-shipping-your-device))

### Removing your account before shipping

**This is critical and must be done before shipping:**

**iPhone:**  
Settings → [Your Name] → Sign Out → Enter Apple ID password → Sign Out.  
Then: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content and Settings.

**Android:**  
Settings → Accounts → Remove Google Account.  
Then: Settings → General Management → Reset → Factory Data Reset.

**MacBook:**  
Apple Menu → System Preferences → Apple ID → Sign Out.  
Then: Apple Menu → System Preferences → Erase All Content and Settings.

> ⚠️ **Failure to remove your account is the #1 cause of buyer disputes on Go2Hand.** An iCloud or Google-locked device cannot be set up by the new owner — this will result in a dispute and forced return at your expense.

## 11. Shipping Your Device

### How to add a tracking number

1. Go to **Dashboard → Orders**
2. Find the sale and click **"Mark as Shipped"**
3. Select your shipping provider
4. Enter the tracking number exactly as shown on your receipt
5. Confirm

The buyer is notified automatically and can track the package.

### Packing guidelines

Proper packing protects you from "damaged in transit" disputes.

**You will need:**
- Bubble wrap or foam padding
- A sturdy cardboard box (original device box is ideal)
- Strong packing tape
- A printed or handwritten label with the buyer's full address

**How to pack:**
1. Wrap the device in bubble wrap — at least 2–3 layers for screens
2. If including the original box, place that inside a larger shipping box with padding around it
3. Do not ship the device in just the original retail box — it needs an outer protective layer
4. Secure all seams with strong tape
5. Write the tracking number on the outside of the package before handing to the courier

### Recommended couriers in Vietnam

| Courier | Strengths | Typical delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| **J&T Express** | Wide coverage, affordable | 1–3 days |
| **ViettelPost** | Reliable tracking, good rural coverage | 2–4 days |
| **GHTK** | Fast for HCMC and Hanoi routes | 1–2 days |
| **GHN (Giao Hàng Nhanh)** | Excellent e-commerce integration | 1–3 days |
| **Ninja Van** | International capability | 2–5 days |

Keep your shipping receipt until the order is completed — it is your proof of dispatch if a dispute arises.

### Shipping costs

Shipping is **free for buyers** on Go2Hand. As a seller, you absorb the shipping cost — factor this into your price when listing. Average domestic shipping for a smartphone in Vietnam is ₫25,000–₫50,000 depending on distance.

## 12. Getting Paid

### When do I receive my money?

Payment is released to you after one of these events:

1. **Buyer approves the device** — clicks "Approve & Release Payment" during the 5-day inspection window
2. **5-day window expires** — if the buyer takes no action, payment is auto-released to you

Once released, your payout is processed within **1–3 business days** to your registered bank account.

### Platform fee

Go2Hand charges **5%** of the selling price. This is deducted from your payout automatically.

```
Example:
Selling price:       $650
Platform fee (5%):  -$32.50
───────────────────────────
Your payout:         $617.50
```

### What if the buyer disputes?

If the buyer raises a dispute during the 5-day window, your payment is held while Go2Hand investigates. You will be asked to provide:

- Proof of shipment (courier receipt, tracking confirmation)
- Photos of the device before packing
- Any communication with the buyer

If the dispute is resolved in your favour, payment releases to you. If resolved in the buyer's favour, the hold on their card is cancelled (they are not charged) and they return the device to you.

## 13. Reviews & Seller Reputation

After a sale completes, buyers are prompted to leave a review rating:
- **Overall experience** (1–5 stars)
- **Seller communication** (1–5 stars)
- **Device accuracy vs listing** (1–5 stars)

Your average ratings are displayed on your seller profile and influence buyer decisions for every future listing.

### How to build a great seller reputation

- **Be accurate** — grade condition honestly and disclose any defects
- **Respond quickly** — reply to buyer questions within a few hours
- **Ship fast** — ship within 1–2 business days of the sale (not the maximum 3)
- **Pack carefully** — devices damaged in transit reflect poorly on sellers
- **Remove accounts before shipping** — this eliminates the most common complaint

### Verified Seller status

Go2Hand awards **Verified Seller** status to sellers who meet a combination of:
- Completed sales threshold
- Average rating above 4.5
- Identity verification submission
- Listing accuracy history

Verified Sellers receive a shield badge on their profile and listings, which significantly increases buyer trust and conversion rates. Apply at [/get-verified](/get-verified).

## 14. Tips for Selling Faster

Based on Go2Hand sales data, these factors consistently lead to faster sales and better prices:

### Photo quality is everything
Listings with 8–10 high-quality photos in natural light sell **3× faster** than minimum-photo listings. Buyers judge condition by photos before reading the description.

### Price within the suggested range
Listings priced at or below the suggested market price sell **2× faster**. Buyers on Go2Hand comparison-shop — if your price is 15% above comparable listings, they will buy from someone else.

### Grade honestly (not optimistically)
Sellers who consistently grade one level higher than the actual condition generate disputes, bad reviews, and slower future sales. An honest B listing sells faster than a disputed A listing.

### Complete the IMEI check
Verified listings with a ✓ Clean IMEI badge receive 40% more views than unverified listings. The check takes 30 seconds.

### Write a real description
A genuine personal description — why you're selling, how you used it, what accessories are included — converts browsers into buyers. Buyers can sense authenticity.

**Good description example:**
> *Bought this in January 2024 as my work phone. Upgrading to the S25 now. Always kept in a case with screen protector — back is pristine, front has micro-marks only visible at a very specific angle in sunlight. Battery health is 91% which is excellent for 14 months of use. Comes with original Apple 20W charger, lightning cable (never used), and the original box with all inserts.*

**Poor description example:**
> *Good phone. Selling because upgrade. No scratches. Good battery.*

### List during peak hours
Most buyers browse Go2Hand between 7–9pm on weekdays and 10am–2pm on weekends. If your listing goes live during these windows, it appears higher in the "Newest" sort order when buyers are actively searching.

## 16. Seller FAQ

**Q: Is listing free?**  
A: Yes. There are no listing fees, no subscription charges, and no upfront costs. Go2Hand takes 5% only when your device sells.

**Q: How many devices can I list?**  
A: There is no limit. You can list as many devices as you own. Sellers with multiple active listings may be eligible for Verified Seller status.

**Q: What if I sell the device somewhere else before it sells on Go2Hand?**  
A: Deactivate the listing immediately from **Dashboard → My Listings**. If a buyer has already placed an order, contact Go2Hand support to cancel — do not simply ignore the order.

**Q: Can I set a minimum offer price?**  
A: Not currently. Buyers can send you an offer via the "Make an Offer" button, and you can accept or decline. A minimum offer feature is on the product roadmap.

**Q: What happens if the buyer claims the device is different from the listing?**  
A: Go2Hand reviews the dispute. You will be asked to provide your original listing photos, proof of shipment, and photos of the device before packing. If your listing was accurate and honest, disputes are typically resolved in the seller's favour.

**Q: Can I cancel an order after the buyer has paid?**  
A: You should not cancel confirmed orders unless there is a genuine issue (e.g. the device was damaged before shipping). Contact Go2Hand support immediately if you need to cancel. Frequent cancellations negatively affect your seller rating.

**Q: When do I remove my account from the device — before or after the sale?**  
A: Before shipping. Sign out and factory reset the device before packing it. Do not wait until after the buyer confirms receipt — the buyer cannot set up the device with your account still active.

**Q: The buyer hasn't approved after 5 days — what happens?**  
A: Payment is automatically released to you after the 5-day inspection window expires. No action is needed on your part.

**Q: What if the buyer says the device was damaged in transit?**  
A: Keep your shipping receipt and photos of the device before packing. If you packed properly and used a reputable courier, this is typically covered by the courier's insurance. File a claim with the courier and provide documentation to Go2Hand.

**Q: Can I list accessories only, like cables or cases?**  
A: Currently, Go2Hand is focused on whole devices (smartphones, laptops, tablets, watches, audio devices). Accessories-only listings are not supported at this time.

**Q: How do I become a Verified Seller?**  
A: Visit [/get-verified](/get-verified) to submit your identity verification and apply. Requirements include a minimum number of completed sales and a high average rating.

**Q: I entered the wrong IMEI. Can I change it?**  
A: Contact Go2Hand support at support@go2hand.vn to correct the IMEI on an active listing. Do not create a duplicate listing.

**Q: What currencies does Go2Hand use?**  
A: All prices are listed and paid in USD (US Dollars). This provides consistency for cross-border comparisons and simplifies Stripe's payment processing.
