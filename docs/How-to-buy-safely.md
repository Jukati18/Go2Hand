## 3. Understanding Device Listings

Every listing on Go2Hand must include specific information. Here is what each field means.

### Listing Title
The brand, model, and storage capacity — for example:  
*Apple iPhone 13 Pro 256GB Natural Titanium*

### Photos
A minimum of 5 real photos are required, including:
- Front screen (on and off)
- Back panel
- Sides and corners
- Any scratches or wear

> ⚠️ **Tip:** If photos look like stock images from the internet, or there are fewer than 5, be cautious. You can contact the seller to request additional photos before purchasing.

### Battery Health
For smartphones, this percentage is pulled directly from device settings. As a general guide:
- **90–100%** — Excellent, like new battery
- **80–89%** — Good, will last a full day for most users
- **70–79%** — Acceptable, may need charging mid-day on heavy use
- **Below 70%** — The listing is required to disclose this clearly

### Storage Capacity
The total internal storage of the device (e.g. 128GB, 256GB). This cannot be expanded on most modern phones.

### iCloud / Google Account Lock
The listing must state whether the device is **Unlocked** from the previous owner's cloud account. A locked device cannot be set up — do not purchase a device listed as "iCloud Locked" unless you have explicit written confirmation from the seller that they will remove the lock before shipping.

### Carrier Status
Whether the device is **carrier-unlocked** (works with any SIM card) or locked to a specific network. Most listings on Go2Hand are unlocked.

## 4. The IMEI Verification System

**IMEI** (International Mobile Equipment Identity) is a unique 15-digit number assigned to every mobile device. Think of it as a passport for your phone.

### Why it matters
A stolen or blacklisted phone can be remotely blocked by carriers, making it unable to connect to any mobile network. Without IMEI verification, you could unknowingly buy a device that stops working after a few weeks.

### What Go2Hand checks
Before a device can be listed for sale, its IMEI is verified against:
- **Global stolen-device databases** — phones reported stolen in Vietnam and internationally
- **Carrier blacklists** — phones flagged by network operators
- **Format validity** — using the industry-standard Luhn algorithm to detect fake or altered IMEIs

### What the result means

| Status | Meaning | Should I buy? |
|---|---|---|
| ✓ **Clean** | Not reported stolen, not blacklisted | ✅ Yes, safe to proceed |
| ⚠️ **Flagged** | Found in a stolen or blacklisted database | ❌ Do not purchase |
| — **Not Verified** | IMEI check not completed | ⚠️ Proceed with caution — contact seller |

### How to check an IMEI yourself
You can cross-check independently at any time:
1. Ask the seller for the full 15-digit IMEI
2. On iPhone: dial `*#06#` or go to Settings → General → About
3. On Android: dial `*#06#` or go to Settings → About Phone
4. Visit [imei.info](https://www.imei.info) or [checkmend.com](https://www.checkmend.com) for a free check

## 5. Condition Grades Explained

Go2Hand uses a four-grade system to describe the physical and functional condition of every device. These grades are assigned honestly by sellers and verified through the listing review process.

### Grade A+ — Like New

The device shows no visible signs of use. It has been handled only a handful of times, if at all.

**What to expect:**
- No scratches, scuffs, or marks visible under any lighting
- Screen in perfect condition with no blemishes
- Original accessories (charger, cable, box) may be included
- Battery health typically 95–100%

**Best for:** Buyers who want a near-new experience at a significant discount.

---

### Grade A — Excellent

Light, everyday wear that is only noticeable under very close inspection or harsh lighting.

**What to expect:**
- Possible micro-scratches on the back panel (not visible from a normal distance)
- Screen in perfect or near-perfect condition
- All buttons, speakers, cameras, and ports fully functional
- Battery health typically 85–95%

**Best for:** Most buyers — excellent value with minimal cosmetic compromise.

---

### Grade B — Good

Normal use marks that are visible but do not affect the functionality or screen quality.

**What to expect:**
- Light scratches on the back, sides, or corners
- Screen is crack-free but may have light surface marks
- Full functionality — all features work correctly
- Battery health typically 75–85%

**Best for:** Buyers prioritising price over cosmetics, or those who immediately use a case.

---

### Grade C — Fair

Noticeable cosmetic wear that shows the device has been used regularly over time.

**What to expect:**
- Visible scratches, scuffs, or minor dents
- Possible screen wear (no cracks unless explicitly disclosed)
- Full functionality — all core features work
- Battery health typically 65–80%
- Price reflects the cosmetic condition — significant discount vs retail

**Best for:** Budget buyers who need a working device and don't mind visible wear.

---

> **Important:** Any cracks — whether on the screen or the body — **must be disclosed** by the seller, regardless of grade. If you receive a cracked device that was not mentioned in the listing, this is grounds for a dispute and full refund.

## 6. How Escrow Payments Work

Escrow is the core safety mechanism of Go2Hand. Understanding it clearly will give you confidence every time you buy.

### What is escrow?
Escrow means a **trusted third party holds your money** until both buyer and seller have fulfilled their obligations. Go2Hand, via Stripe, acts as that trusted third party.

### The exact flow

```
1. You enter your card details at checkout
        ↓
2. Stripe AUTHORISES (holds) your card — no money moves yet
        ↓
3. Seller ships the device with a tracking number
        ↓
4. You receive the device and begin the 5-day inspection window
        ↓
    ┌── You APPROVE → Stripe CAPTURES the payment → Seller receives funds
    │
    └── You DISPUTE → Go2Hand reviews → Stripe CANCELS the hold → Card refunded
```

### Key point: you are never charged and left without a device

The hold on your card is **cancelled** (not refunded — because you were never actually charged) if a dispute is resolved in your favour. This typically appears as the pending charge disappearing from your bank statement within 1–5 business days depending on your bank.

### What "authorised but not captured" means on your bank statement
After checkout, you will see a **pending transaction** on your card. This is the authorisation hold. It is not a completed charge. The funds do not leave your account until you explicitly approve the device — or until the 5-day inspection window expires (at which point the system releases automatically on the assumption that no news is good news).

## 7. Your 5-Day Inspection Window

After you click **"I Received the Device"**, a 5-day countdown begins. This is your protected time to thoroughly test everything.

### The countdown

The timer is visible on your order page, showing days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining. The status changes colour as the deadline approaches:
- **Green** — plenty of time
- **Amber** — less than 24 hours remaining
- **Red (pulsing)** — less than 1 hour remaining

### What happens when the window expires?
If you take no action within 5 days, the payment is **automatically released to the seller**. This is intentional — if there were a serious problem, buyers act quickly. The auto-release protects sellers from indefinite delays.

> ⚠️ **Do not click "I Received the Device" until the package is physically in your hands and you have had a chance to examine it.**

### What if the device hasn't arrived yet?
Do not click the received button until it actually arrives. If the tracking shows delivery but you haven't received it, contact your building management or neighbours first. If the device is genuinely lost or undelivered after delivery confirmation, open a dispute.

## 8. How to Inspect Your Device

Use this checklist during your 5-day window. Take your time — this is your protected period.

### Physical Inspection

- [ ] **Screen** — check for cracks, deep scratches, dead pixels, and yellowing at corners
- [ ] **Back panel** — compare scratches/scuffs to the listing photos
- [ ] **Corners and edges** — check for dents or bends not shown in photos
- [ ] **Buttons** — press every button (power, volume, mute switch if applicable)
- [ ] **Speaker grilles** — no visible blockage or damage
- [ ] **Camera lenses** — clean, no cracks or fogging
- [ ] **Ports** — lightning/USB-C port is clean and undamaged
- [ ] **SIM tray** — present and undamaged

### Software and Functionality

- [ ] **Power on** — device turns on and boots normally
- [ ] **iCloud / Google lock** — you should be able to set up the device with your own account without any "Activation Lock" screen appearing
- [ ] **Battery health** — check Settings → Battery → Battery Health (iPhone) or a battery app (Android) and compare to the listed percentage
- [ ] **Wi-Fi** — connects and browsing works
- [ ] **Mobile data** — insert your SIM and confirm it connects to your carrier
- [ ] **Bluetooth** — pairs with another device
- [ ] **Cameras** — test front and rear cameras, including video
- [ ] **Speakers** — play audio, check for crackling or distortion
- [ ] **Microphone** — record a video or make a test call
- [ ] **Face ID / Touch ID** — set up and test biometric unlock
- [ ] **Flashlight** — test the torch function
- [ ] **Screen touch** — test all areas of the screen, including corners

### For Laptops

- [ ] **All keyboard keys** — use a key-test website (e.g. keyboardtester.com)
- [ ] **Trackpad** — click, scroll, and gesture support
- [ ] **Screen** — check for dead pixels and backlight bleed
- [ ] **Charging** — plug in and confirm the battery charges
- [ ] **Ports** — test each USB, HDMI, and headphone port
- [ ] **Webcam and microphone** — test with a video call or recording app
- [ ] **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth** — confirm both work
- [ ] **Battery life** — use the laptop unplugged for at least 30 minutes to get a realistic sense

## 10. Opening a Dispute

If the device does not match the listing — wrong condition, missing accessories that were listed, undisclosed damage, or iCloud/Google lock still active — you can open a dispute during the 5-day inspection window.

### When to dispute (valid reasons)
- Device condition is significantly worse than the stated grade
- Screen has a crack that was not disclosed in the listing
- Battery health is meaningfully lower than listed (more than 10% difference)
- Device has an active iCloud or Google Account lock
- Wrong device or wrong storage capacity received
- Device does not power on or has a major functional fault
- IMEI shows flagged even though listing said clean

### When NOT to dispute
- You changed your mind about the purchase (use the return policy instead)
- Minor cosmetic differences consistent with the stated grade
- You forgot to click "Received" and the window expired

### How to open a dispute

1. Go to **Dashboard → My Orders** → find your order
2. Click **"Raise a Dispute"**
3. Write a clear description of the problem (minimum 10 characters, but the more detail the better)
4. Submit — your payment hold is now protected while Go2Hand reviews

### What happens during a dispute

1. Go2Hand receives your dispute and the seller is notified
2. Both parties may be asked to provide evidence (photos, screenshots)
3. Go2Hand reviews the case — typically within 24 hours
4. If resolved in your favour: the payment hold is cancelled and your card is refunded
5. If resolved in the seller's favour: payment is released to the seller

> **Tip:** Take clear photos of any issues as soon as you find them. Timestamped photos are powerful evidence in a dispute.

## 11. Refunds & Returns

### Dispute refunds (within 5-day window)
If a dispute is resolved in your favour, your card is refunded. Because escrow holds an authorisation rather than a completed charge, the hold is simply cancelled — your money never left your account. The pending charge disappears from your bank statement within 1–5 business days.

### 30-day return policy
Changed your mind after approving? Go2Hand offers a 30-day return window.

**To initiate a return:**
1. Contact the seller via your order page
2. Agree on return shipping (buyer typically pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns)
3. Ship the device back in the same condition it was received
4. Once the seller confirms receipt, your refund is processed

**Return conditions:**
- Device must be in the same condition as received
- All accessories that came with the device must be included
- The 30-day period begins from the delivery date

### What cannot be returned
- Devices returned in worse condition than received
- Returns initiated after 30 days from delivery
- Devices where the serial number has been altered

## 12. Red Flags to Watch For

Even with Go2Hand's protections, it pays to recognise warning signs before purchasing.

### 🚩 In the listing
- Fewer than 5 photos, or photos that look like official press images rather than real device photos
- Price dramatically below market value (more than 40% under typical second-hand prices)
- Vague or copy-pasted description with no personal details
- IMEI status not shown or listed as "Not Verified"
- Condition grade does not match the photos shown

### 🚩 From the seller
- Seller has no reviews or very few completed sales
- Seller asks you to complete the transaction outside of Go2Hand (via bank transfer, Zalo Pay, etc.) — **never do this**, you lose all protection
- Seller is unresponsive or slow to reply to questions
- Seller pressure to approve quickly before you have had time to inspect

### 🚩 When the device arrives
- Box or accessories appear different from what was listed
- Device shows signs of water damage (check the SIM tray for a red indicator)
- An "Activation Lock" screen appears when you try to set up the device
- Battery health is significantly lower than stated
- The IMEI on the device does not match the IMEI on the box or in the original listing

> If you encounter any of these after receiving the device, **do not approve the payment**. Open a dispute immediately.

## 13. Buyer FAQ

**Q: Is my card charged when I place an order?**  
A: No. Your card is authorised (a hold is placed), but you are not charged until you explicitly approve the device or the 5-day inspection window expires. If you dispute and win, the hold is simply cancelled.

**Q: What happens if the seller never ships?**  
A: If the seller does not ship within a reasonable time, contact Go2Hand support. You can cancel an order before it ships and your authorisation hold will be released.

**Q: Can I see the seller's past reviews before buying?**  
A: Yes. Click on the seller's name in any listing to view their public profile, including their overall rating, number of completed sales, and all reviews from past buyers.

**Q: What if I can't inspect the device within 5 days (e.g. I'm travelling)?**  
A: Do not click "I Received the Device" until you are ready to inspect it. The 5-day window only starts when you manually trigger it. You can wait as long as needed before pressing that button — the tracking will show delivery, but the inspection window won't start automatically.

**Q: Can I negotiate the price?**  
A: Yes. Use the **"Make an Offer"** button on any listing to send the seller a custom price offer. The seller can accept, decline, or counter.

**Q: What if the seller's account disappears after I pay?**  
A: Your money is held in escrow by Stripe, not by the seller. Even if a seller's account is suspended, your funds are safe and Go2Hand will arrange a refund.

**Q: Is Go2Hand available outside of Vietnam?**  
A: Currently, Go2Hand serves buyers and sellers within Vietnam. Shipping is domestic only.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
A: All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) via Stripe. Additional payment methods may be added in future.

**Q: I approved the payment by accident. What do I do?**  
A: Once approved, the payment is final. Contact Go2Hand support immediately — in some cases, if the seller has not yet received their payout and there is a legitimate issue, we may be able to help. This is why we recommend using the full 5-day window before approving.

**Q: How do I contact Go2Hand support?**  
A: Visit the [Contact page](/contact) or email support@go2hand.vn. For urgent disputes, open a dispute directly on your order page — this creates an automatic case with the support team.
